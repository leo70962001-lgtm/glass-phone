Top-down view straight down into a calm, crystal clear Japanese garden pond with no fish. Gentle sunlight caustic light patterns shimmer and slowly ripple across the pebbles on the pond bottom, soft small ripples travel across the water surface, the floating lily pads and red maple leaves bob and drift very slightly on the water. Slow, calm, peaceful, natural motion, like a quiet afternoon. The camera is completely static: no zoom, no pan, no rotation. The layout, colours and every object stay in the same place the whole time. No fish, no koi, no animals, no people, no text.
