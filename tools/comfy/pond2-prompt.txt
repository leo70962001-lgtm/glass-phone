Top-down painterly view of a calm koi pond with no fish. The turquoise and teal water flows slowly: soft lighter aqua swirls and currents drift gently across the picture, shimmering light caustics move over the water, and delicate ripple rings slowly spread outward and fade. The round lily pads and white water lilies float and bob very slightly in place. Slow, calm, peaceful motion, like a quiet painted pond coming to life. The camera is completely static: no zoom, no pan, no rotation. The layout, colours and every lily pad and flower stay in the same place the whole time. No fish, no koi, no animals, no people, no text.
