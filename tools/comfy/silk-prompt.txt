Abstract wallpaper of soft silk ribbons made of coloured light. The ribbons slowly ripple and undulate, gentle waves travel along them from the lower left to the upper right, and a soft glossy sheen glides across the silk folds. Smooth, calm, slow motion, dreamy and elegant. The camera is completely static: no zoom, no pan, no rotation. The colours, layout and composition stay the same the whole time: pink and magenta on the left, deep blue at the upper right, a silver-white ribbon across the middle, orange-red at the lower right. No text, no objects, no people.
